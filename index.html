lab-quality-toolkit ↗

Laboratory quality calculations in Python

A tested package for the calculations an ISO/IEC 17025 lab does every week: GUM uncertainty budgets with Welch–Satterthwaite coverage factors, method validation (linearity, LOD/LOQ), proficiency-test z, zeta and En scores, Westgard control rules, and flue-gas emission correction to reference O₂.

energy-forecasting-dashboard ↗

Day-ahead energy demand forecasting

Hourly demand for residential, school, factory and EV-charging loads, with leak-free features and a five-fold rolling backtest. XGBoost cuts error by 30–48% against the better of two naive baselines, and the README explains where linear models fail. Includes a Streamlit dashboard, tests and CI.

f1-podium-prediction ↗

Formula 1 podium prediction

Trained on 2010–2022 and tested on 90 unseen races, using pre-race information only. It shows how much of a first version's apparent accuracy came from leaked post-race data, and how little models add over the starting grid.
